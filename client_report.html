Client / Developer Team Project Report

Client Report

As a client, I decided what content and images to use by focusing on what best represents me and the purpose of my website. I selected simple and clear information, along with images that matched the overall theme of the site.

Overall, this was a very pleasant experience. I did not have any major issues during the project. My team members were responsible and did their part well, which made the process much smoother.

At the beginning, there was a small lack of communication, which caused a bit of confusion. However, we were able to resolve it quickly by improving how we communicated and making sure everyone understood their responsibilities.

My developer met my expectations. The final website was organized, visually clean, and followed the structure of my site plan.

If I worked with a team again, I would try to establish clearer communication from the beginning to avoid any initial confusion.

Lead Developer Report

As a lead developer, I managed my team by assigning tasks and making sure each member understood their responsibilities. I guided the junior developers on what pages to create and helped maintain consistency across the website.

During the project, I faced a challenge with my client, as they became unresponsive and I was not able to contact them. Because of this, I had to take on both the client and lead developer roles, as instructed by the professor.

Since we had already completed a large part of the project, my team and I decided to continue using the same website. However, we made adjustments and modifications based on what we thought was most appropriate and functional.

I continued communicating with my team to ensure we stayed on track and completed the project successfully despite the situation.

I was responsible for publishing the site using GitHub Pages. I managed the repository, uploaded the files, and shared the final version of the site.

This experience helped me improve my problem-solving and leadership skills, especially in unexpected situations where I had to adapt and take more responsibility.

Junior Developer Report

As a junior developer, I worked on two different team projects. In the first project, everything went very smoothly. Communication was clear, and the team worked well together. In the second project, we faced a bigger challenge because our lead developer became unresponsive. As a result, one of my teammates, who was also a junior developer, stepped into the lead role.

Despite this situation, we worked very well as a team. We stayed organized, communicated effectively, and were able to continue making progress. Even with time constraints, we managed to move forward quickly and complete our work.

Through these experiences, I learned how to adapt to unexpected situations, collaborate with different team dynamics, and stay responsible for my tasks.

General Experience and Reflections

The most challenging part of this project was dealing with unexpected situations, such as communication issues and team members becoming unresponsive. However, I was able to overcome these challenges by adapting quickly, taking initiative, and maintaining good communication with the rest of the team.

What I enjoyed most about this project was the opportunity to work in a team and experience how real-world web development projects function. It was interesting to see how different roles interact and depend on each other.

If I could do this project again, I would focus on establishing clearer communication from the very beginning and setting expectations early to avoid confusion later on.

Throughout this project, I improved my HTML and CSS skills, especially in structuring pages, organizing content, and maintaining consistency in design across different pages.

I also developed stronger collaboration and problem-solving skills. I learned how to adjust when plans change, work with different types of teammates, and continue making progress even in challenging situations.

After completing this project, I feel more confident working on real-world projects because I now understand both the technical and teamwork aspects involved in web development.
